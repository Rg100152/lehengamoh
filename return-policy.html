Return & Refund Policy

Exchanges and refunds made simple • Last updated: November 2024

We want you to love your purchase. If something is not right, our return and refund process is designed to be simple and fair.

Return Window

Eligible items may be returned within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unworn, unwashed and have all original tags and packaging intact.

Non-Returnable Items

Customised or altered pieces, and items marked as final sale, cannot be returned. For hygiene reasons, certain accessories may also be non-returnable.

How to Start a Return

Contact our support team with your order ID and reason for return. We will guide you through the pickup or self-ship process.

Refunds

Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method. For cash-on-delivery orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer.

Exchanges

If you need a different size, we will arrange an exchange subject to availability. If the requested size is unavailable, a refund or store credit will be offered.

Shipping for Returns

Return shipping is free for damaged or incorrect items. For other returns, a nominal shipping fee may be deducted from the refund.

Contact

For any return-related questions, email support@lehgamoh.com or use our Contact page.
