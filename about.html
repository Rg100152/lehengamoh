About Lehgamoh

Since Day One

Crafting elegance, weaving traditions.

Lehgamoh began with a simple idea: that every woman deserves an outfit that feels as extraordinary as the moment she is celebrating. From the first sketch to the final stitch, each piece is created with intention, patience and respect for the craft.

We work closely with skilled karigars across India, combining time-honoured techniques like zardozi and hand embroidery with contemporary silhouettes. The result is ethnic wear that feels heirloom-worthy yet completely at home in the modern wardrobe.
