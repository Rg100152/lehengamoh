Shipping Policy

Delivery timelines and charges • Last updated: November 2024

Everything you need to know about how and when your Lehgamoh order will reach you.

Order Processing

Orders are carefully packed and processed within 2–3 business days. You will receive an order confirmation as soon as your order is placed.

Delivery Timelines

Standard delivery within India usually takes 5–7 business days after dispatch. Remote locations may take slightly longer. Timelines are estimates and not guarantees.

Shipping Charges

Shipping is free on all orders above ₹5,000. Orders below this amount are charged a flat rate of ₹199, which is shown clearly at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you can track its status using the Track Order page with your order ID.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address and phone number are correct. Lehgamoh is not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by incorrect address details.

Delays

Occasionally, deliveries may be delayed due to weather, festivals or courier issues. We will keep you informed and do our best to resolve any delay quickly.

International Shipping

At present we ship only within India. International shipping is not available.
