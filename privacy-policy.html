Privacy Policy

How we handle your information • Last updated: November 2024

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used when you shop with Lehgamoh.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to share with us, including your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order history. This information is used solely to provide and improve your shopping experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your details are used to process orders, arrange delivery, respond to enquiries and, if you opt in, send updates about new collections. We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

Local Storage on This Demo

Lehgamoh is a demonstration website built with static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Account, cart, wishlist and order information is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to a server.

Managing Your Data

You can clear your locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data. This will remove your saved account, cart, wishlist and order history from that browser.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and this demo does not process real payments.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. For data stored locally, you can delete it directly through your browser settings.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please email support@lehgamoh.com or use our Contact page.
