Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you shop.

How do I place an order?
Browse the collection, open a product, choose your size and click Add to Cart. When you are ready, open your bag and proceed to checkout. Fill in your shipping details and place the order.
What payment methods do you accept?
This is a demo store, so no real payment is taken. The checkout supports Cash on Delivery, Card and UPI options for demonstration purposes only.
How long does delivery take?
Orders are processed within 2–3 business days and usually delivered within 5–7 business days across India. Free shipping applies to orders above ₹5,000.
Can I return or exchange an item?
Yes. Eligible items can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unworn and have original tags. See our Return Policy for full details.
How do I choose the right size?
Use our Size Guide to compare your bust, waist and hip measurements with each size. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up or contacting us for help.
Is the fabric handcrafted?
Yes. Every Lehgamoh piece is finished by hand in small batches using traditional techniques such as zardozi and hand embroidery.
Do you ship internationally?
At the moment we only ship within India. International shipping is planned for the future.
How do I track my order?
Open Track Order from your account or the footer, then enter your order ID. You will see the current delivery status and timeline.
Can I cancel or modify an order?
Orders can be changed before they are dispatched. Please contact support as soon as possible with your order ID.
How do I contact support?
You can call us at +91 98765 43210, email support@lehgamoh.com, or use the contact form on our Contact page.
